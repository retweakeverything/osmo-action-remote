## What's new

- **Wake the camera from the app.** Sleep and Wake are now normal commands on
  the Remote and camera pages, and a new **Wake** block is available in
  workflows.
- **ESP32 relay route.** Plug an ESP32 into the phone over USB and choose
  **ESP32 on USB** on the Connect screen. The app sends the same commands
  through it, and the ESP32 sends the wake signal from a fixed Bluetooth
  address, which the camera requires. A phone on its own normally cannot.
- **Ready-to-flash ESP32 firmware** for ESP32, ESP32-C3 and ESP32-S3 is
  attached to this release.
- The app links to the firmware download and flashing guide.

## Notes

- Waking needs camera firmware **01.04.08.10 or later** (Settings > About on
  the camera; update with DJI Mimo).
- Pairings are now kept per route. After updating, the camera asks you to
  confirm once more on the phone route, and once for the ESP32.
- The app asks for one more Bluetooth permission (advertising), used for the
  wake broadcast on the phone route.
